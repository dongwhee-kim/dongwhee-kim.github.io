## **ECC-ExerSim (Error-Correcting Code Exercise and Simulator)**

- Korea Copyright Commission, No. C-2023-043210
- Study the fundamentals of Classical Error Correcting Codes (e.g., Hamming Code, Reed-Solomon Code) through practical experimentation and develop experimental methodologies to evaluate the reliability of memory systems based on these principles.
